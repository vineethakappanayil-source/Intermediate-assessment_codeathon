# 📊 Power BI Sales Data Analysis

## Overview
An interactive **Power BI Sales Data Analysis Dashboard** created to clean, analyze, and visualize sales data using **Power Query and DAX**.

## 🛠️ Tools
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## 🔹 Data Cleaning
- Handled missing values
- Removed duplicate rows
- Standardized OrderDate format
- Loaded the cleaned data into Power BI
## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Dashboard
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Unit Price
- Profit Trend Over Time
- Orders by Region
- Top 5 Products by Orders
- Sales & Profit by Category
- Year filter

### 2. Product & Sales Analysis
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Total Cost
- Sales by Product
- Orders by Product
- Profit by Product
- Category and Product filters
- Product-level analysis

### 3. Regional Sales & Profit Analysis
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Orders by Region

### 4. Insights & Recommendations
- Sales performance insights
- Profit performance insights
- Product performance insights
- Regional performance insights
- Business recommendations
- ### 5. Product Details – Drill-through

A dedicated **Drill-through page** is included for detailed product-level analysis.

- Product-level Sales
- Product-level Profit
- Quantity
- Orders
- Product performance details

Users can right-click a product in the main dashboard and select **Drill through → Product Details** to view detailed information for the selected product.
## 📈 Visualizations
- Order distribution by Region – Pie Chart
- Top 5 Products by Orders – Column Chart
- Profit Trend over Time – Line Chart

## 🧮 DAX
- **EastRegionOrders** – Calculated table for East region orders
- **ProfitCostDifference** – Calculated column for Profit − Cost
- **TotalProfit** – Measure for total profit
